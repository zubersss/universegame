# M3 report: Mercury, Venus, tools and trade (2026-11-02)

**What you can do in this build.** Everything from M2, plus two new planets and the
player's kit: Mercury, with its burning day side, a slow-moving twilight band, solar
flares that come with a 45-second warning and flare shelters to hide in, and Solarite
crystals that can only be mined for 90 seconds after a flare; Venus, where you live on
floating stations above the acid clouds and make timed dives to the hot surface for vent
glass; the Rig multitool (mining beam, lance, scanner, builder); crafting at the smelter
and fabricator; an inventory and a catalogue; trading for Scrip at the Earth and Mars
Waystations; the Wardens (hostile drones that wake if you dig too much near their sites);
base lamps that light shelters at night; and the first boss creatures (the Cinderhulk on
Mercury, the Veil Serpent on Venus).

**How to run it.** Open `C:\dev\universegame\Build\MERIDIAN_0.3.0` and double-click
`MERIDIAN.exe`. Controls are the same as before; J holds the Skip Drive (not yet needed).

**What to try (15–30 min).** Fly to Mercury, walk toward the twilight band and wait for a
flare warning, then get into a shelter. Mine Solarite right after. Fly to Venus and land on
the station deck. Craft a radiation liner. Sell something at a Waystation.

**What I need from you.** Tell the Lead in plain words what felt wrong, confusing, slow,
or ugly.

**What's next.** M4: the gas giants (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) with no ground,
floating ice floes, the Skip Drive, ring mining, and the giants' creatures and bosses.
