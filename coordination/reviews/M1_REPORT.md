# M1 report: the first playable loop (2026-10-30)

**What you can do in this build.** Start a new game on Earth, walk, jump, and dig. Board
your ship, launch on real thrust, fly across the solar system map to Mars, feel its gravity
pull you in, enter its atmosphere, land by hand, and save. Art is a mix of finished and
placeholder pieces. There are no creatures, story, or crafting yet; those come in M2 and M3.

**How to run it.** Open the folder `C:\dev\universegame\Build\MERIDIAN_0.1.1` and
double-click `MERIDIAN.exe`.

**Controls.**
- On foot: A/D walk, Shift run, Space jump (hold it in the air for the jetpack), mouse
  aims, left click digs, right click places a block, F boards the ship.
- Ship on a planet: W thrust up, S slow down, A/D sideways, F gets out once landed.
- In space: W thrust, A/D turn, Shift boost. Mars is straight to the right of Earth.
- Esc pauses and saves.

**What to try (15–30 min).** Land on Mars gently (watch V/S on the left; under 6 is
clean), then try a hard landing. Jump on Mars and compare it with Earth. Save, quit, and
use Continue.

**What I need from you.** Tell the Lead in plain words what felt wrong, confusing, slow,
or ugly. You don't need to explain why.

**What's next.** M2: real scenery, day and night, creatures, the Mars dust storm.
