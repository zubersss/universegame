# M2 report: Earth and Mars, finished look and feel (2026-10-31)

**What you can do in this build.** Everything from M1, now in a world that looks finished:
a new title screen; a starting camp by the ship; Earth's six regions (forest, plains,
desert, ocean, northern forest, tundra) with matching scenery; day and night; stags and
wolves on Earth, scavengers and canyon hunters on Mars; landmarks (Survey Archive, launch
site, crashed hauler, Warden outpost); Mars's canyon, Olympus Mons, ice caps and lava
tubes; dust storms with a 60-second warning; cold that drains your suit at night; engine
flames, dig sparks and landing dust; and all eight planets on the space map.

**How to run it.** Open `C:\dev\universegame\Build\MERIDIAN_0.2.0` and double-click
`MERIDIAN.exe`. Controls are the same as M1.

**What to try (15–30 min).** Walk west to the Survey Archive (130 m) and east toward the
desert and ocean. Stay out at night and watch the suit bar. Fly to Mars, land near the
canyon and jump down its ledges. Wait for a dust storm.

**What I need from you.** Tell the Lead in plain words what felt wrong, confusing, slow,
or ugly.

**What's next.** M3: Mercury and Venus, the player's tools, crafting, inventory, trading
at the Waystations, Wardens, and the first boss creatures.
